# Anti-slop audit 001

Date: 2026-09-15
Scope: Kadoya House landing page, `app/page.tsx` and `app/globals.css`
Mode: AFTER

## Findings

1. **HIGH · R-02 Copywriting**
   The visible hero kicker contains an em dash in `Est. 1924 —`. R-02 forbids em dashes in UI text. Replace it with a period, colon, or a typographic separator that is not an em dash.

2. **HIGH · R-17 Data & Numbers**
   The stats section presents `1924`, `700`, `8`, and `50+` as factual numbers without an attached source. R-17 requires real, verifiable sources or no numbers. Keep only numbers the owner confirms, or label them explicitly as placeholders while they are awaiting verification.

3. **HIGH · R-25 Color Contrast**
   The hero text sits over a photographic background with a changing wash, and the location label sits over an image. Contrast may vary across the image. These areas need contrast testing across the full text region, with a stronger local backing treatment if any point fails WCAG AA.

4. **HIGH · R-32 Keyboard Accessibility**
   The lightbox can be opened and closed with keyboard-focusable controls and Escape, but focus is not moved into the dialog or returned to the invoking gallery button. Add dialog focus management and a visible focus path so keyboard users do not lose context.

5. **MEDIUM · R-26 Interactive Elements**
   The three “Sekitar” links all point to the generic contact section rather than a destination specific to the selected activity. This technically scrolls, but the action does not match the labels. Give each item a useful destination or make the labels clearly indicate that they start an inquiry.

6. **MEDIUM · R-38 Real Content or Honest Placeholder**
   The stay descriptions and nearby destinations are described as an “initial overview,” but the cards otherwise read as final accommodation and destination claims. Since the user requested dummy content, mark these items visibly as “Contoh informasi” or “Segera dikonfirmasi” until real details are supplied.

7. **LOW · R-04 Icons**
   UI symbols such as `▣`, `◇`, `▶`, `↓`, `→`, and `↗` are used as repeated iconography. Their purposes are understandable in some controls, but the repeated generic glyph treatment should be reduced or replaced with content-specific ornamental marks so the colonial visual language feels authored rather than symbol-driven.

8. **LOW · R-08 Button Arrows**
   Arrows appear in several action labels, including the accommodation button and nearby links. Keep an arrow only where it communicates movement or opening a detail, and remove it from actions where it is purely decorative.

9. **LOW · R-06 Typography**
   The type choices are directionally appropriate for the colonial brief, but the navigation uses a serif plus wide tracking at very small size. Verify readability at the primary 300px viewport and reduce tracking or increase size if labels become fragile.
